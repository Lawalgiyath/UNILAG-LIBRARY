How access works

Three ways in.

Most databases

University IP

On campus, the University IP address carries your entitlement. Click the logo and the provider opens. No individual login.

Research4life

Username and password

AGORA, HINARI, OARE, GOALI and ARDI are reached by username and password only. For credentials, contact or visit the Automation Unit.

Emerald

Register for remote

Emerald Insight is reached through the University IP address on campus. For full text off campus, an individual must register.

IP access

ScienceDirect

More than 15 million scientific articles, of which over 900,000 are open access, peer reviewed and reusable.

Open on ScienceDirect IP access

iG Publishing

One of the leading providers of ebook collections, with over 150,000 titles covering all disciplines.

Open iG Publishing Register for remote

Emerald Insight

Scholarly journals in management, business, education, library studies, health care and engineering.

Open Emerald Credentials

Research4life

AGORA for agriculture, HINARI for health, OARE for environment, GOALI for legal and ARDI for intellectual property.

Open Research4life IP access

HeinOnline

Comprehensive coverage from inception of more than 3,300 multidisciplinary periodicals, government documents, constitutions and world trials.

Open HeinOnline IP access

JSTOR

A digital library of more than 2,000 journals, nearly 20,000 books and two million primary source objects.

Open JSTOR IP access

ProQuest Central

The largest multidisciplinary full text database. Over 100,000 full text dissertations and 455,000 working papers from NBER, OECD and others.

Open ProQuest IP access

ASME Digital Collection

Journals from 1933 to present, conference proceedings from 2000 plus select back to 1955, and ebooks from 1993.

Open ASME IP access

Brill

Over 360 journals, 8,000 volumes and 150,000 articles back to the year 2000, in three subject collections.

Open Brill IP access

Springer Nature

Access to a world of science, technology, medicine, social sciences and humanities.

Open Springer Nature IP access

ProQuest Ebook Central

Ebooks across business, health and medical, social sciences, arts and humanities, education, science and religion.

Open Ebook Central IP access

IJMSO

International Journal of Mathematical Sciences and Optimization, hosted by the University of Lagos.

Open IJMSO IP access

JaypeeDigital Medical

More than 3,500 health science books, journals and procedure videos, with 350 new products each year.

Open JaypeeDigital IP access

Cambridge University Press

Cambridge Core, with more than 380 peer reviewed journals and more than 33,000 ebooks.

Open Cambridge Core IP access

HSTalks

The Biomedical and Life Sciences Collection of audio visual lectures from leading scientists.

Open HSTalks
