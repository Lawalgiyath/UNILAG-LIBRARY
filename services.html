01

Loan Request

Borrowing privileges for circulating books and other library resources applies to duly registered library users only. While circulating books are available for open shelf retrievals, books in closed access areas are available for users who wish to use such materials within the library. There are two categories of loan: short term loan and long term loan.

Long term loan

All circulating resources are available for long term loan.

Interlibrary loan

Interlibrary loan is available for both print and electronic materials.

Short term loan

Resources available for short-term loan include the Reserved Book Room collection, journals and other periodicals, reference materials, audio-visual collection, theses and dissertations, archival and special collections, government publications and newspapers, past examination question papers, and laptops.

03

Circulation

The Circulation Desk is where borrowing, returns, renewals, recalls, library card registration, fines and holds are handled. It is also the first place to ask when a book cannot be found on the shelf.

Borrowing limits
Undergraduates4 items
Postgraduates10 items
Staff10 items
Loan duration

Undergraduates may borrow for 2 weeks and renew up to two consecutive times. Staff and postgraduates may borrow for 1 month and renew up to two times after the due date stamped on the book.

Library card. Your student identification card grants access to the library, but you need your library card to check out or renew books. Cards are issued at the Circulation Desk, Faculty of Education Library or College of Medicine Library, and are non transferable.

05

Support for Users with Disabilities

The University Library renders inclusive library services to users with disabilities. Our service offering includes:

  • Provision of assistive tools on students’ personal devices
  • Scan and convert course materials to braille or audio
  • Transcription and conversion of resources to accessible format
  • Editing of materials in accessible formats
  • Research assistance, by appointment with the Reference or Faculty Liaison Librarians
  • Reader assistance, reading citations, abstracts and other materials
  • Continuous library orientation and information literacy programme
  • Extended loan periods
  • Provision of document delivery service
  • Provision of a Visually Impaired Resource Centre
  • Provision of ramps for users in wheelchairs

06

Reprographic

Photocopying, printing and reproduction of library materials for staff and students, handled within the library under the University’s copyright rules.

Ask at the Circulation Desk for the current reprographic schedule and charges.

07

Bindery

The University Library has a well established bindery that offers high standard binding services to staff, students, academic departments and private clients. Services include repair and restoration of books and other print materials.

08

Where to Publish: Accredited & High-Impact Journals

A specialized research guidance service and search tool for University of Lagos lecturers, academic faculty and doctoral researchers to discover verified, accredited publishing venues.

  • Search verified journals indexed in Scopus, Clarivate Web of Science (WoS), DOAJ, and African Journals Online (AJOL)
  • Filter by UNILAG faculty discipline, CiteScore metrics, and Quartile rankings (Q1 – Q4)
  • Access automated publisher manuscript finders (Elsevier JournalFinder, Springer Nature Suggester, Wiley, IEEE, and WoS Matcher)
  • Consult the "Think. Check. Submit." checklist to safeguard against predatory and cloned journals
  • Guidance on Article Processing Charge (APC) waivers and Read & Publish open access agreements

01  /  3rd Floor • Media Production & Digital Scholarship

The Multimedia Studio

A premier acoustic recording and digital broadcasting facility established on the 3rd Floor of the Main Library for scholarly podcasts, expert interviews, and educational video productions.

  • Multi-seat podcast interview suite with broadcast condenser microphones on adjustable boom arms
  • Warm acoustic timber-slat wall paneling, architectural backlighting, and studio overhead lighting grid
  • Ideal for research dissemination, doctoral defense interviews, educational course recording, and student creative projects
02  /  Collaborative Computing

The Library Learning Commons

The technology nerve center of the Main Library, seamlessly combining digital computing terminals, team study spaces, and on-site information desk guidance.

  • Ergonomic PC clusters with high-speed internet and direct access to ScienceDirect, JSTOR, and UNILAG IR
  • Wall murals celebrating vision, innovation, and academic growth ("Speak", "Innovation", "Success")
  • Dedicated on-duty librarians at the Information Desk for immediate reference assistance and technical support

03  /  Ideation & Innovation

The Library Makerspace

Located on the first floor of the Main Library, the Makerspace is purpose-built to inspire creative problem solving, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and design thinking.

  • Iconic "MAKE IDEAS HAPPEN" lightbulb mural and mobile smart interactive boards
  • Circular discussion lounge with plush curved seating designed for group ideation and hackathons
  • Presentation projection screen and flexible workshop layout for student innovator groups
