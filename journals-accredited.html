Publication Integrity & Standards

Avoiding Predatory Journals: Think. Check. Submit.

Predatory and cloned journals target academics with unsolicited emails promising rapid publication without rigorous peer review. Publishing in predatory outlets invalidates promotion papers and compromises research credibility.

Verify Editorial Board: Confirm that the editor-in-chief and editorial board members are recognized authorities with authentic institutional affiliations.
Verify Direct Indexing: Never trust the badges on a journal website. Always verify the journal's active indexed status directly on Scopus Sources or the WoS Master List.
Clear Peer-Review Guidelines: Legitimate scholarly venues have a documented, double-blind or transparent peer review process that takes adequate time for independent expert assessment.
Transparent Publication Fees: Genuine open access journals clearly state their Article Processing Charges (APCs) upfront before submission, never after acceptance.
