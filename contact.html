Non members

Visitors from outside the University

Non members of the university can gain access on presentation of a letter of introduction and valid identification card from their institution or employer. An admittance card would be issued following registration and payment of a required fee at the Main Library Circulation Desk.

Members

Students and staff

Students and staff of the University of Lagos gain access with the university identification cards. The ID card does not serve as a borrower’s card. Borrowing requires a library card, issued at the Circulation Desk, Faculty of Education Library or College of Medicine Library.
