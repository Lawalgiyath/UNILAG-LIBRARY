Learn About Us

A library built
for the long work.

The University Library’s vision is to be the primary and foremost source and vessel of reliable information for the University community in its quest to realise its noble vision concerning teaching, research and community services.

The Library sets out to provide information services to meet the intellectual needs of members of the University community, and to make information seeking most rewarding and enjoyable for the various categories of users in the community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you
come in.

Students and staff of the University of Lagos can gain access with the university identification cards. However, the ID card does not serve as borrower’s card. Non-members of the university can gain access on presentation of a letter of introduction and valid identification card from their institution or employer. An admittance card would be issued following registration and payment of a required fee at the Main Library Circulation Desk.
Use the Traditional Card catalogue or the Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC). Each contains materials held by the Library. If you cannot find what you need in the catalogue, please seek assistance at the Circulation Desk.
  • Undergraduates: 4 items
  • Postgraduates: 10 items
  • Staff: 10 items
Students pay twenty naira per item for each day the item is late, while staff pay fifty naira per item per day after a loan period of six months. A lost item is replaced with a recent edition, or charged at the current price with an additional processing fee of one thousand naira.
