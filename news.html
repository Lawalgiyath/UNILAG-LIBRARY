Library Multimedia Studio at the Main Library

Launched on 19th January 2026 on the 3rd Floor of the Main Library. Equipped with broadcast condenser microphones on adjustable boom arms, acoustic timber soundproofing, and overhead studio lighting, the new studio opens up opportunities for academic podcasts, video lectures, scholarly interviews, and creative student productions.
