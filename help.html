FAQs

Frequently asked
questions.

Students and staff of the University of Lagos can gain access with the university identification cards. However, the ID card does not serve as borrower’s card. Non-members of the university can gain access on presentation of a letter of introduction and valid identification card from their institution or employer. An admittance card would be issued following registration and payment of a required fee at the Main Library Circulation Desk.
Use the Traditional Card catalogue or the Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC). Each contains materials held by the Library. If you cannot find what you need in the catalogue, please seek assistance at the Circulation Desk.
Seek assistance at the Circulation Desk or from library personnel in the reading areas if you have difficulties locating a book on the shelf.
You may seek assistance of the Reference Librarian if the book you need is unavailable in the library. The library may request to borrow a copy of the item from another library via its Inter-Library Loan Service or purchase it.
There are several reasons you may not be able to check out a book. The most common are:
  • The item is not loanable. Examples of such items are print reference and other specialized materials, which may be used within the Library only.
  • You have not returned an item that is either overdue or has been recalled for another library user, or is on reserve. If any of these reasons apply, please return the item as soon as possible.
  • You owe fines or fees on overdue borrowed books. You are expected to make a payment towards the amount you owe, or pay the balance in full.
  • Visit the Circulation Desk to report the problem, and seek assistance on how to remedy it.
Your student identification card only grants you access to use the library, but you need your library card to check out or renew books. You may place a book on hold pending when you complete your library registration and present your library card to check out loanable items.
Visit the Circulation Desk at the Main Library, Faculty of Education Library or College of Medicine Library.
University of Lagos Library Cards are non transferable. Your card will be confiscated if anyone other than yourself attempts to use your card to borrow library materials.
  • Undergraduates: 4 items
  • Postgraduates: 10 items
  • Staff: 10 items
Undergraduates may borrow for a period of 2 weeks and renew up to two consecutive times. Staff and postgraduates may borrow for 1 month and renew up to two times after the due date stamped on the book.
Visit the Circulation Desk to renew borrowed books. You may renew items checked out to you up to two times if no reservation is placed on the item.
You may not be able to renew a book if another user has placed a hold on it, or if it has been recalled for reserves. You may also be prevented from renewing a book if your borrowing privileges have been suspended for any reasons.
Submit your request at the Circulation Desk to place a recall on it.
No. Please do not re-shelve items. Return them either to a marked shelving station or leave them lying on a table.
Library fines and charges vary. For overdue fines, students pay twenty naira per item for each day the item is late, while staff pay fifty naira per item per day after a loan period of six months. If an item is lost, the library will accept replacement of the item with a recent edition, or charge a replacement fee which is dependent on the current price of the item, with an additional processing fee of one thousand naira (N1000.00).
Start the payment procedure at the Circulation Desk and make payment via Remita into the University account.
