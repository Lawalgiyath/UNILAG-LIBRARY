Accessibility
& inclusion.

The University Library renders inclusive library services to users with disabilities.

11 ProvisionsVisually Impaired Resource Centre
PL. 03 / Within the library
What we provide
Assistive tools
Provision of assistive tools on students’ personal devices
Braille and audio
Scan and convert course materials to braille or audio
Accessible formats
Transcription and conversion of resources to accessible format
Editing
Editing of materials in accessible formats
Research assistance
By appointment with the Reference or Faculty Liaison Librarians
Reader assistance
Reading citations, abstracts and other materials
Orientation
Continuous library orientation and information literacy programme
Extended loans
Longer loan periods for users who need them
Document delivery
Provision of document delivery service
Visually Impaired Resource Centre
Provision of appropriate accommodation
Ramps
Provision of ramps for users in wheelchairs
