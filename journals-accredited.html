Academic Integrity & Safeguards

Safeguarding Your Research: Think. Check. Submit.

Predatory and cloned journals aggressively solicit manuscripts via unsolicited emails. Publishing in unaccredited journals invalidates promotion submissions and damages academic reputations.

  • Verify Peer-Review: Confirm that the journal offers clear, documented peer review guidelines and an identifiable editorial board of recognized scholars.
  • Cross-Check Direct Indexing: Do not rely on claims on the journal website. Search for the ISSN directly on the official Scopus Sources portal or Web of Science Master List.
  • Check Retraction & Ethics Policy: Ensure the publisher is a member of COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics) or OASPA.
  • Assess Fee Transparency: Legitimate open access journals clearly state Article Processing Charges (APCs) before submission, never requesting surprise post-acceptance fees.
